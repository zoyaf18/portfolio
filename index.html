the work

I'm Zoya, a software engineer, working on enterprise AI platforms and developer tools that help teams create AI agents, bring intelligent capabilities into their products, and rethink how they work.

the why

Working at the intersection of generative AI, platform engineering, and product experience has deepened my interest in how emerging technology can translate into meaningful user experiences. I'm drawn to the decisions behind the product: understanding what people need, weighing technical trade-offs, shaping features, and collaborating across teams to turn complex capabilities into experiences that feel intuitive.

the dots

I enjoy the engineering, and I'm equally fascinated by the human side of it: the conversations, the different perspectives, and the small insights that change how a problem is understood. I love connecting those dots and turning them into a direction people can get behind.

off-screen

Off-screen, I'm usually somewhere between a good book, an unfinished idea, storytelling, and exploring new places. I'm always curious about the latest in AI and emerging tech, and a rabbit hole traveler on completely random topics.
